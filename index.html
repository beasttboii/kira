The key idea: WhatsApp can display HTML documents in a restricted viewer. Because that viewer limits some external requests, a page may appear different from how its creator designed it.

01 / THE BASICS

How the viewer works

Think of an HTML file as a small webpage. It can contain text, layout, images, and interactive elements.

01
↗

A document is shared

Someone sends an HTML document in a WhatsApp conversation.

→
02
▣

It opens in a viewer

WhatsApp opens the document in a restricted web-viewing environment.

→
03
◌

Some content may be limited

Requests to domains the viewer does not allow may be blocked.

🔒

Privacy detail: WhatsApp says web content opened this way could be accessible to Meta, so it is not protected by end-to-end encryption. Your personal messages and calls remain end-to-end encrypted.

02 / WHAT YOU MIGHT NOTICE

Why a page can look incomplete

It does not automatically mean the file is broken or your phone has a problem.

▧Images, icons, or fonts are missingThey may be hosted on a domain the viewer does not permit.
▤The layout or styling looks unfinishedStylesheets or other design resources may not load.
↗Some links or controls do nothingInteractive features can depend on blocked resources.
▦Embedded content does not appearCharts, maps, and embedded items may not load.
GOOD TO KNOW
✦

A different display doesn't always mean damage.

The viewer may simply be preventing the document from loading resources from certain external locations.

03 / WHAT TO DO

View shared documents carefully

A few sensible steps can help you choose how to view a document.

✓

Know the sender

Only open HTML documents from people or organizations you recognize and trust.

!

Don't assume it's verified

WhatsApp notes that its secure viewer cannot confirm whether a document is safe.

PDF

Ask for another format

If the page is incomplete, ask the sender to share the information in a message or as a PDF.
